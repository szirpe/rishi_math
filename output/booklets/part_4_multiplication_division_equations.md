# 37  Combine 𝑥 groups before solving

Simplifying like terms can reveal an easier equation.

Worked example:

$$
\begin{array}{lcl}
2\mathit{x} + 3 + \mathit{x} & = & 18 \\[6pt]
(2\mathit{x} + \mathit{x}) + 3 & = & 18 \\[6pt]
3\mathit{x}       + 3 & = & 18 \\[6pt]
3\mathit{x}       + 3 - 3 & = & 18 - 3 \\[6pt]
3\mathit{x} & = & 15 \\[6pt]
\frac{3\mathit{x}}{3} & = & \frac{15}{3} \\[6pt]
\phantom{3}\mathit{x} & = & 5
\end{array}
$$
