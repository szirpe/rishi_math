# Part 1 of 4

## Single side expressions

Sort matching terms and combine them while keeping the same value.

**What you will practice**

- Understand 𝑥 and count copies of 𝑥.
- Group numbers together and 𝑥 terms together.
- Work from filled examples to your own steps.

**Symbols:** 𝑥 is the unknown number. × means multiply.

**How to work**

Read the example, try the blanks, then choose your own steps as the help fades. Work at your own pace. Use the scratch space whenever you need it.

Keep unchanged terms under themselves and line up your equals signs.

**Ready for the next part when**

I can simplify an expression and explain why only matching terms combine.
