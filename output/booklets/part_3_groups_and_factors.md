# 24  Connect groups to 𝑥

3𝑥 + 2𝑥 counts five groups of 𝑥. Put 𝑥, the common factor, before the brackets. At the end, 𝑥 × 5 becomes 5 × 𝑥, written 5𝑥.

Worked example:

```text
3𝑥      + 2𝑥
(𝑥 × 3) + (𝑥 × 2)       write 𝑥 first in each group
𝑥 × (3 + 2)             common factor first
𝑥 × 5                   add inside brackets
5 × 𝑥                   reorder multiplication
5𝑥                      write in short form
```

# 25  Multiply and divide groups

× means multiply. A fraction bar means divide: 24/4 means 24 divided by 4. The whole quantity above the bar is divided by the number below it. 4 × 𝑥 and 4𝑥 mean the same thing.

Worked example:

```text
3 × 4   + 2 × 4
(4 × 3) + (4 × 2)       write the shared factor first
4 × (3 + 2)             common factor first
4 × 5                   add inside brackets
20                      multiply
```

# 28  Divide a whole group

When the entire sum is divided, each term can be divided. The brackets tell you what belongs to the group.

Worked example:

$$
\begin{array}{lcl}
\frac{(12 + 8)}{4} & &  \\[6pt]
\frac{12}{4} & + & \frac{8}{4} \\[6pt]
3 & + & 2 \\[6pt]
5 & &
\end{array}
$$

**1. (18 + 12)/6**

$$
\begin{array}{lcl}
(\frac{\underline{\hspace{0.8cm}}}{6}) & + & (\frac{\underline{\hspace{0.8cm}}}{6}) \\[6pt]
\underline{\hspace{0.8cm}} & + & \underline{\hspace{0.8cm}} \\[6pt]
\underline{\hspace{1.4cm}} & &
\end{array}
$$
