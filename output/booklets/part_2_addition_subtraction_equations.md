## Addition and subtraction equations

An equation is balanced. Keep it balanced by doing the same operation on both sides.

**What you will practice**

- Subtract or add on both sides and recognize zero pairs.
- Simplify each side and solve with 𝑥 on either side.
- Check your answer in the original equation.

**Symbols:** 𝑥 is the unknown number. × means multiply.

**How to work**

Read the example, try the blanks, then choose your own steps as the help fades. Work at your own pace. Use the scratch space whenever you need it.

Keep unchanged terms under themselves and line up your equals signs.

**Ready for the next part when**

# 08  Meet the balanced equation

The equals sign says both sides have the same value. Subtract the same amount from both sides.

Worked example — subtract 3 on both sides, then cross out zero pairs:

$$
\require{cancel}
\begin{array}{rllcllll}
\mathit{x} & +3 & & = & 1 & +2 & +3 & \\
\mathit{x} & \cancel{+3} & \cancel{-3} & = & 1 & +2 & \cancel{+3} & \cancel{-3} \\
\mathit{x} & & & = & 1 & +2 & & \\
\mathit{x} & & & = & 3 & & &
\end{array}
$$

# 16  Undo subtracting by adding

To undo -3, ADD 3 on both sides. The pair -3 + 3 equals zero.

Worked example — the struck pair equals zero:

$$

\begin{array}{rllcll}
\mathit{x} & -3 & & = & 5 & \\
\mathit{x} & \cancel{-3} & \cancel{+3} & = & 5 & +3 \\
\mathit{x} & & & = & 8 &
\end{array}
$$
